As Mars celebrates its 115-year journey, we sat down with Theresa McCulla, Mars Museum Curator , to discuss the rich, surprising and far-reaching history of the business. From our humble beginnings in a Tacoma kitchen to scanning the world's very first barcode and launching beloved treats into orbit, Theresa shares the fascinating stories behind some of the company’s most iconic moments, innovations and global legacy.
