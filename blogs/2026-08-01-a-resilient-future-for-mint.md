Behind every stick of chewing gum is a supply chain that few people ever think about. Yet behind one of Mars' most important ingredients lies a story of long-term investment, collaboration and innovation that is helping to improve livelihoods, empower women, protect natural resources and build a more resilient supply chain. Menthol is a critical ingredient in many of Mars' chewing gum and mint products.
