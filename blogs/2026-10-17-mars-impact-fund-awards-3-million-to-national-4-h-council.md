Mars Impact Fund is providing $3 million over three years to support National 4-H Council’s Beyond Ready program, designed to give youth the skills they need to succeed in a rapidly changing world. The partnership will support up to 200,000 young people across the United States in pursuing career paths in food, agriculture and veterinary sciences.
