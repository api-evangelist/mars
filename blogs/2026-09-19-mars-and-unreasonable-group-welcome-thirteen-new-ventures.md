Mars and Unreasonable Group welcome thirteen new ventures to Unreasonable Food™ As an international network, Unreasonable Food is uniquely positioned to scale high-growth ventures dedicated to creating a more sustainable food system. NEW YORK, NY (September 14, 2026) – Today, as part of the Unreasonable Food™ collaboration, Mars and Unreasonable Group announced the thirteen ventures joining the 2026 cohort, the third since the program launched in 2024.
