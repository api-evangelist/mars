More than half of European workers would switch jobs for a pet-friendly workplace A new survey by Mars, Incorporated of 16,000 workers in Europe reveals that more than a third (36%) of workers prioritize pet-friendly policies over traditional workplace perks and other benefits 71% of workers who would be more likely to get a pet if their workplace offered more supportive pet-friendly policies, agree they would consider switching jobs if pets were allowed at work
