At Mars, our work has been guided by a simple belief: a better world for pets creates a better world for people. Today, fulfilling that purpose means tackling one of the primary reasons pets end up in shelters: the lack of accessible, pet-inclusive housing. We believe access to pet-friendly housing is crucial for families to stay together with their pets.
