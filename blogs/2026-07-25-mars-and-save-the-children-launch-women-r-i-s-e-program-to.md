The Women R.I.S.E. Program was officially kicked off at the North Kolaka Regency Bappeda Hall. Initiated by Save the Children Indonesia and the Mars Impact Fund, this program aims to strengthen the resilience of cocoa farming communities and reinforce the child protection system in North Kolaka Regency, Southeast Sulawesi.
